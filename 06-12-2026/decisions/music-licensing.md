# Decision: Music Licensing

## Decision

Use procedural placeholder music in-app for now, and document CC0 candidates for the first real downloadable music pass.

## Why

The CC0 OpenGameArt candidates have simple license obligations and fit the prototype. The itch.io Dark Fantasy pack is useful, but CC BY 4.0 adds attribution and change-note obligations that should be handled intentionally before bundling.

## Sources Reviewed

- OpenGameArt, Medieval: The Bard's Tale by RandomMind, CC0.
- OpenGameArt, Forest Ambience by TinyWorlds, CC0.
- itch.io, Music loops pack 1 - Dark Fantasy by Chris "Torone" CB, CC BY 4.0.
- Creative Commons CC0 and CC BY license summaries.

## Next

Add real audio files only after the repo has an `ASSET_LICENSES.md` file that records source URL, author, license, and attribution text for every bundled asset.
