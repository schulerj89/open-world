# Decision: Simple Circle Colliders

## Decision

Use simple horizontal circle colliders for the player against town objects, NPCs, enemies, and streamed tree trunks.

## Why

The current art style is low-poly and the game already streams procedural objects. Circle colliders are cheap to generate, cheap to resolve, and easy to expose in debug. They also avoid per-mesh collision checks against many small procedural meshes.

## Coverage

- Town buildings, fences, props, market stalls, statue base, and NPCs.
- Live meadow slimes.
- Tree trunks generated by visible terrain chunks.

## Debugging

The HUD reports recent collision pushes and visible tree blocker count. The `0 Collide` quick tool intentionally places the player against a cottage blocker so collision can be verified in the browser without walking across the map.
